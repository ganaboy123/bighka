UI/UX LAB / 03 · ONBOARDING CONCEPT

FORM

A clear first step for something new, explored through friendly copy and a lightweight onboarding flow.

01 / THE STUDY

Make the first step feel simple.

Form is a lightweight onboarding concept built around clear language and a one-step-at-a-time rhythm. The sample screen introduces the experience and asks for a single detail to get started.

STUDY TYPEOnboarding concept
DESIGN INTENTClear first step
VISUAL TONEFriendly · focused
STATUSPortfolio exploration

02 / DESIGN DIRECTION

Explain the step before asking.

The visual pairs a short welcome with one sample field, a clear action, and a small progress cue. This is a concept screen, not a functioning onboarding flow.

AT A TIME

03 / WHAT THE CONCEPT EXPLORES

Welcome, guide, continue.

A visual exploration of how simple language and visible progression can make a starting point feel less demanding.

01 — WELCOME

Set a warm tone

A brief opening message helps frame why the flow begins here.

02 — INPUT

Ask one thing

The sample asks for a single detail, keeping the first interaction focused.

03 — PROGRESS

Show what’s ahead

A small step indicator signals that the first screen is part of a larger flow.

04 / NEXT TO VALIDATE

Open questions.

  • Which product and user need this onboarding is meant to serve.
  • What information is essential and why it is requested.
  • Validation, error, privacy, and accessibility states.
  • How many steps the complete flow needs and how users respond to it.
