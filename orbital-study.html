UI/UX LAB / 02 · FINANCIAL PRODUCT CONCEPT

ORBITAL

An exploratory finance interface using hierarchy and a simple overview to make information feel approachable.

INTERFACE STUDYSTATUS · CONCEPT
EXPLORE THE STUDY ↓

01 / THE STUDY

Make the big picture easier to navigate.

Orbital explores a financial overview organized around one prominent summary, a simple trend visualization, and a secondary balance row. The figures in the mock screen are sample content only, not real financial information or performance.

STUDY TYPEFinancial interface concept
DESIGN INTENTApproachable overview
VISUAL TONESimple · structured
STATUSPortfolio exploration

02 / DESIGN DIRECTION

Lead with the overview.

The concept prioritizes a clear summary and supporting trend, with secondary information lower in the visual hierarchy. It is not connected to a financial service.

03 / WHAT THE CONCEPT EXPLORES

Clarity through hierarchy.

The screen study considers how a finance overview can feel organized without requiring the viewer to interpret a dense table first.

04 / NEXT TO VALIDATE

Open questions.

  • Which user group and financial tasks this interface should support.
  • Terminology, privacy needs, and required account states.
  • Accessible chart descriptions and responsive information order.
  • Usability feedback; all displayed figures are illustrative placeholders.
