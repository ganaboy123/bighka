01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Make complex readings feel clear.

The project brief describes a device concept for analyzing chemical compositions and presenting relevant information. Its stated aim is to make complex readings easier to understand.

02 / CONCEPT DIRECTION

From analysis to understanding.

The visual direction treats the reading as the beginning of an explanation. This is a design exploration of that intent, not a confirmed interface or product feature.
