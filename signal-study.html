01 / THE STUDY

Give daily priorities room to breathe.

Signal is an interface exploration for seeing priorities and progress at a glance. The design direction uses a quiet hierarchy and a small number of summary areas to keep the dashboard approachable.

STUDY TYPEDashboard concept
DESIGN INTENTDaily focus
VISUAL TONECalm · clear · compact
STATUSPortfolio exploration

02 / DESIGN DIRECTION

One clear starting point.

The preview groups a greeting, focus summary, project count, and a small weekly graphic. These are illustrative interface values and content, not live account data or a tested product.

03 / WHAT THE CONCEPT EXPLORES

Less noise, useful signals.

A visual study of how a dashboard might make daily information feel easier to scan.

01 — HIERARCHY

Start with today

A direct greeting and focus summary create a clear first read.

02 — PROGRESS

Keep metrics small

Compact figures provide a quick sense of progress without dominating the page.

03 — RHYTHM

Scan the week

A simplified chart gives the interface a lightweight time-based overview.

04 / NEXT TO VALIDATE

Open questions.

  • Who the dashboard is for and what decisions it should support.
  • Which information is most useful in the first view.
  • Accessibility, responsive behavior, and real data requirements.
  • Usability feedback; this concept has not been tested as a product.
